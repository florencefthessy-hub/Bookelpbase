Behind the Pages

I’m Kelvin Ojochegbe, a reader first and the founder of Bookelpbase. Behind every book on a shelf is a person with a story about how it came to be, and I wanted a place where those stories could be told properly.

Bookelpbase hosts online interviews with authors about their books: the spark that started them, the long nights of writing, and the characters that would not leave them alone. Each spotlight is written from the book itself. When an author answers my questions, their words appear in full and unedited.

If you have written a book and would like its story told, I would love to hear from you.
